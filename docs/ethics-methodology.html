YGN Methodology

Campaign-Finance Transparency Grade

What the Grade Means

The campaign-finance grade is an educational transparency signal from 0 to 100, converted into a familiar letter grade. It is not an accusation, legal conclusion, endorsement, or misconduct finding.

Published grades use current FEC campaign-finance data and the method version shown in the data record. If YGN cannot match enough current evidence, it displays N/A. It does not fill the gap with a synthetic, randomized, or data-completeness grade.

Live Formula

  • Small-donor share, 30%: higher share of unitemized individual contributions improves the score.
  • PAC independence, 25%: lower PAC and party-committee dependence improves the score.
  • Donor diversity, 20%: lower reliance on itemized donations of $2,000 or more improves the score.
  • Self and party independence, 15%: lower reliance on candidate funding and party committees improves the score.
  • In-state support, 10%: more itemized support from the represented state improves the score.

Stock-Conflict Deduction

  • House members: YGN counts recent House Periodic Transaction Report filings that disclose individual-stock or asset trades.
  • Deduction: 0 filings subtracts 0 points; increasing activity subtracts 10, 22, 32, 42, or at most 50 points from the campaign-finance score.
  • Unavailable measurement: when a machine-countable disclosure feed is unavailable, no stock deduction is inferred.

Refresh Cadence

The scheduled static-data workflow spends a bounded FEC request budget and reuses current-method live grades for up to 25 days. It preserves the original evidence timestamp when reusing a grade, so an old result cannot become “fresh” merely because a new build ran. Public page requests prefer the committed evidence-backed snapshot and do not consume FEC quota by default.

Grade Bands

A is 93 and above, A- is 90 to 92.9, B grades run from 80 to 89.9, C grades from 70 to 79.9, D grades from 60 to 69.9, and F is below 60.
